VIC Property Hub demo × PropertyME

AU residential PM mini‑SaaS — first‑cut study

A page to forward: where the VIC Property Hub browser demo sits against PropertyME, and what the servers might cost if the wedge became a product. Researched 28 October 2026. USD list prices unless marked AUD.

01Thesis

Three lines a principal can repeat. The rest of the page is the evidence.

  1. Do not clone PropertyME. Their Manage PM page publishes 1.9M+ properties, 6,400+ agencies, and 2 out of 3 Australian investment properties. Trust accounting is the core of that product, described there as woven in rather than bolted on.1

  2. Trust is the buy. End-of-month money ops is the highest-liability job in the agency. A cleaner task list does not replace a trust ledger, an owner statement, or a disbursement run.

  3. The wedge is VIC-first, small rent rolls, or one workflow sharp enough that an agency keeps PropertyME and still pays for this.

Next bets, in this order

  1. Trust-lite. Receipt, then allocate, then owner payout and statement.

  2. VIC notice and compliance pack. A calendar that knows which check is due.

  3. Keep the portals and the maintenance approval already sketched in the demo.

  4. Leave the full ERP parked until bet 1 is real. Listings, a trades marketplace, and an inbox product wait with it.

The sharp slice already in the demo is one property file: arrears, a bond note, inspection acknowledgement, and landlord approval on a job.

02Pain → function

Why an agency pays, and the function that answers that pain. The order matches the bets above.

Trust and end of month

Pain · highest liability

Money ops: receipting, allocation, owner payment, the monthly grind. This is the job that can sink an agency.

Function · bet 1 Trust-lite. Receipt → allocate → owner payout and statement. Audit trail included, or it is not trust-lite.

Compliance and notices

Pain · law that moves

Victorian tenancy rules, notices, and safety checks. A wrong notice is a product defect, not a copy tweak.

Function · bet 2 VIC notice pack and a compliance calendar. Solicitor review before it ships as advice.

The inbox

Pain · owners, tenants, trades

MRI Software’s 2024 Voice of the Property Manager: 47% of property managers work more than 41 hours a week, and 54% report mental-health struggles.2

Function Keep owner and tenant portals so routine status does not become another email. A full inbox product is parked.

Maintenance coordination

Pain · the job stalls

Tenant reports it, landlord has to approve it, someone has to do the work. The handoff is the product.

Function · bet 3 Landlord approve / reject on the job. Already sketched. The stored $500 threshold is not enforced yet. A trades portal waits.

Inspections and renewals

Pain · volume

Check-in, routine, check-out, then the renewal. Repeated on every tenancy.

Function The demo already has the three inspection types plus acknowledge. Mobile polish is a short later step. Renewals wait.

Small-roll economics

Pain · the suite is priced for a bigger desk

PropertyME’s own page describes pricing as transparent.1 Separately, this study records review-site themes (Capterra and similar): reports take too many clicks, advanced setup is hard, pricing feels harsh under about 20–50 doors, and updates sometimes jar. Those themes were not re-quoted for this page.3

Function Scope and price the wedge for a small VIC roll. A discounted clone of the whole suite misses the opening.

04Hub vs PropertyME

Compact read. The PropertyME column follows the public home page and the Manage PM page.1 Where this study names a gap and those pages use different words, the cell says so. GST, RTBA lodgement, and a packaged VIC notice set are in that second group.

Shape of the product

Hub demo One browser workspace. About 27 sample properties. Persona picker. No accounts.

PropertyME Manage PM with Trust Accounting, plus Trustless payments, Grow CRM, and AiMe. Scale claims on the Manage PM page: 1.9M+ properties, 6,400+ agencies, 2 out of 3 Australian investment properties.

Trust and money

Hub demo Rent charges, payments, reversals, bond subledger, RTBA note. No trust ledger, owner statement, disbursement, bank feed, GST, or RTBA lodgement.

PropertyME Trust is described as native, not a bolt-on. The same page names daily bank feeds, two-way reconciliation, real-time owner statements, and bulk disbursements, plus MePay and Trustless payments. GST and RTBA lodgement are study gaps for the Hub; this page does not quote a PropertyME screen for those two.

Inspections

Hub demo Check-in, Routine, Check-out, and acknowledge.

PropertyME Paperless inspections, geo planning, and InspectMe for tenant-completed routine inspections.

Maintenance

Hub demo Priority, landlord approve/reject, $500 threshold stored and not enforced. No trades portal.

PropertyME Jobs: quotes, assign trades, photos, owner and tenant updates.

Portals

Hub demo Landlord sees properties, approvals, arrears. Tenant sees tenancy, payments, and can raise maintenance. No app claimed.

PropertyME Owner access, tenant access, and the PropertyMe app.

Lease, compliance, pipeline

Hub demo Lease fields and status. No VIC notice pack, no compliance calendar, no listings pipeline.

PropertyME Lease renewals. AiMe Comply for state legislation. Compliance tracking for smoke alarms, pools, and insurance. Grow CRM. Listings through to realestate.com.au and other sites. The page also mentions notice automation. A Victorian notice pack under that name is a Hub gap in this study, not a PropertyME product name quoted here.

Messages, files, AI, auth

Hub demo No real email or SMS. Document metadata only. Reports and CSV. No AI module. No server auth.

PropertyME SMS and inbound SMS, chat, activity feed. Documents and key tracking. Dashboard and insights. AiMe reply, AiMe Comply, Bills AI. Cloud product with agency accounts. This study did not review their auth implementation.

People, in AUD, as a wide band

A senior full-stack contractor in Australia often lands around AUD $800–1,500+ a day. It varies wildly. Show the band. Do not turn it into a fake quote. Legal review of trust claims is a separate budget, and it is the one to refuse to skip.

Full ERP stays parked

Bet 4 from the thesis: listings, a trades marketplace, CRM, and a full inbox wait until trust-lite is real. The demo’s property file is the seed. It is not the suite.

07Caveats

What will hurt if it is waved through

  • Trust correctness and the audit trail are the product risk. The screens are the smaller part.
  • Victorian notice wording has to be right. A plausible template is a liability.
  • Migration is a sales cost. Moving off spreadsheets, or off PropertyME, takes people’s trust. PropertyME sells that switch (the Manage PM page describes MigrateMe). Budget the same seriousness in the other direction.
  • Support load. The MRI hours and strain figures are both the reason to build and the warning about who will call.

Disclaimer

This page is a study for a conversation. It is not legal advice, financial advice, tax advice, or a trust-accounting opinion. Trust accounting, owner disbursements, GST, and Victorian notices need a solicitor and an accountant before anyone offers them. Do not call the product “trust accounting” until that review exists.

The VIC Property Hub link is a browser demo. It is not a production SaaS. It does not take signups and it does not hold a real trust account.

Prices are list estimates gathered on 28 October 2026. They will move. Phase bands (~US$45–80, ~US$80–150, ~US$200–600+) are the study’s roll-ups. The worked addition in Phase B is arithmetic on listed lines, labelled as such. Contractor day rates are a market ballpark. Build weeks assume the staffing above and will move when a real scope is written.
